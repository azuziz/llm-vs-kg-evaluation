The distribution of mode-share values indicates generally high output stability across repeated LLM runs, with a substantial proportion of questions yielding identical outputs. However, a smaller subset exhibits considerable variability, suggesting prompt sensitivity or uncertain underlying knowledge. Stability alone does not imply factual correctness and must be interpreted alongside KG agreement metrics.
